Maps

Every path, no signal.

Vector maps of Czechia down to the last footpath and the whole world in overview, drawn on the phone itself, with marked hiking trails on top. The built-in GPS finds you without any network.

Wikipedia

The encyclopedia, offline.

All of the Czech or English Wikipedia on the SD card, searchable in an instant. First aid, a mushroom you just found, the history of the castle on the hill — whenever you need it.

Nature

Every plant and mushroom, named.

A field guide to the plants and fungi of the whole world — every species that has been photographed, with two to five photos you can open full screen, Czech and English names, whether it is edible, medicinal or poisonous and what to do if someone ate it, and where and in which months it grows. Filter by colour, region or season; search works without diacritics.

Translator

Find the word, in thousands of languages.

An offline dictionary between any two languages of your bundle — from a handful of world languages up to every one Wiktionary describes. Words come in their own script with a romanisation, the phrasebook has ready-made sentences, and the phone says them out loud. Longer sentences go word by word, and the app tells you so.

Up to 5,000 languagesSpeaks ~100Every scriptPhrasebook
First Aid

Knows what to do. And what you have.

Made for when no ambulance is coming. Type what happened — a burn, a cough, a wasp, a tick — and get the danger signs, the steps, and what not to do. Keep your medicine cabinet in the phone: it shows which of your medicines help, how many are left and the dose for each person by age and weight, and the herbs that help, with a photo from the field guide and how to make the tea.

~80 guidesDoses by weightMedicine cabinetWorks without a card
Camera

Photos and video, kept on the phone.

A full-screen viewfinder with three zoom steps. Photos and video go straight to the SD card, or to the phone's own memory when there is no card. Voice memos are one tap away.

Messages without towers.

Petdoku

Low Power Mode

A battery that keeps going.

Swipe down for Control Center and tap the leaf: the screen is capped at 30 %, the Wi-Fi chip is switched off completely, the display redraws at 15 frames a second and locks after 30 seconds. At 20 % it turns on by itself. Survival Mode goes further: only Nature, Maps and Radio, and deep sleep the moment the screen goes dark — a double tap wakes it.

      Help

      If something goes wrong.

      Nothing here can break the phone for good — every step can simply be repeated.

      The phone does not show up in the list

      Make sure the cable is in the side port marked P4.U and that it is a data cable; many cables only charge. Try another one if in doubt.

      Close any other program that might be using the phone (Arduino, a serial monitor). Still nothing? Open “For tinkerers” below and tick “Show every device”.

      It cannot connect, or stops half-way

      Put the phone into install mode by hand: hold BOOT, press and release RST, then let go of BOOT. The screen stays dark. Then click Install again.

      An interrupted install is harmless: the phone simply waits for the next one.

      The screen stays dark afterwards

      Do a full power cycle: unplug USB, switch the battery off, wait five seconds, switch it back on and press the power button.

      A nearly empty battery also makes it switch off right away — charge it for a while.

      The phone does not see my files

      Make sure the downloads finished — an unfinished file ends in .crdownload or .part. Copy them to the card, eject the card properly and put it back; the phone sorts them within a few seconds.

      Files larger than 4 GB (English Wikipedia) need a card formatted as exFAT. Cards of 64 GB and more come that way from the shop.

      Wi-Fi does not switch on

      Early phones left the factory with different firmware on their Wi-Fi chip. SurvivalOS replaces it by itself: Settings → WLAN, switch WLAN on and tap Update now. It takes about a minute and is needed only once.

      If it asks for the Wi-Fi chip's BOOT button, hold it, tap Continue, then let go.

      How do I run the setup assistant again?

      On the phone: Settings → More Settings → Run setup again. That is also where you switch between Full and Mini.

      Which phone is this for?

      The LILYGO T-Display-P4 V1.0 (ESP32-P4, chip revision 1.x). The page checks the chip before writing anything and refuses other boards.

      This is an early version: it runs on real hardware but is still growing.

      How do I go back to the original firmware?

      Download the file ending in -merged.bin for t-display-p4-device-v1.0-esp32p4-rev1.0 from the LILYGO releases page, then install it under “For tinkerers” below.
